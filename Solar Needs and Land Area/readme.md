# Solar Needs and Land Area

Made a Plotly Dash dashboard showing how much solar panels are needed for each country based on their approximate  annual electricity usage. 

Check it out here: https://solar-app.plotly.app/

Key Findings:
* Most countries require very little land area replaced with solar to achieve their energy needs.
* Some countries are so small that it is completely impractical to do so.
* This is a proof of concept - several factors prevent this from being practically implemented including but not limited to distribution, cost of materials, availability of usable land and grid scale energy storage availability.
